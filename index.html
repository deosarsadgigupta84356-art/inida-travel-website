India Travel Guide

Visa Requirements

Most foreign nationals need a visa to enter India. Tourist e-Visas are available for citizens of 169 countries, valid for 30 days, 1 year, or 5 years.

International Airports

Major international airports include Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, and Kolkata. Direct flights connect India to major cities worldwide.

Required Documents

Valid passport with at least 6 months validity, visa, return ticket, and sufficient funds for your stay are required for entry.

Indian Railways

India has one of the world's largest rail networks. Book tickets in advance through IRCTC website or app for long-distance travel.

Buses & Taxis

State-run and private buses connect cities. Use app-based taxis like Uber and Ola in metropolitan areas for convenient travel.

Domestic Flights

Multiple airlines operate domestic flights connecting major cities. Book in advance for better fares, especially during peak seasons.

Hotels

Wide range from budget hotels to luxury 5-star properties available in all major cities and tourist destinations.

Homestays

Experience Indian hospitality with homestays, especially popular in Kerala, Goa, and Himalayan regions.

Heritage Properties

Stay in converted palaces, havelis, and forts for a unique experience, particularly in Rajasthan.

Regional Cuisines

Each region has distinct culinary traditions - from rich Mughlai dishes in the north to coconut-based curries in the south.

Vegetarian Options

India offers extensive vegetarian cuisine due to cultural and religious practices. Many restaurants are purely vegetarian.

Street Food

Experience diverse street food but choose vendors with high turnover and ensure food is freshly cooked and hot.

Greetings

Traditional greeting is "Namaste" with folded hands. Handshakes are common in business settings.

Temple Etiquette

Remove shoes before entering temples. Dress modestly covering shoulders and knees. Some temples prohibit non-Hindus.

Gift Giving

Gifts are appreciated but not always opened in front of the giver. Avoid leather items as gifts.
